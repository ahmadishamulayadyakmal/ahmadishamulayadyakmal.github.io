About Me

Background & Overview

I am a metallurgical engineer and LPDP–Tsingshan Group scholar currently pursuing my Master of Engineering at the University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB). With a multidisciplinary electrical–metallurgical background, I combine numerical process modeling (CFD), data-driven analysis of process and laboratory data, and engineering design of process equipment and systems.

My thesis focuses on numerical modeling of the evolution and distribution of non-metallic inclusions in continuous casting of heavy rail steel. I have also completed metallurgical research internships at the Tsingtuo Group Research Institute (Tsingshan Group), analysing inclusions in stainless steel with SEM-EDS.

My academic foundation is in electrical engineering (B.Eng., University of Jember, GPA 3.66/4.00), where my undergraduate thesis on a flyback LED driver was published in a Q3 Scopus journal, the International Journal of Power Electronics and Drive Systems (2023). I was also a recipient of the prestigious Djarum Beasiswa Plus scholarship. In industry, I worked as a Site Support Equipment Group Leader at PT Hasnur Riung Sinergi, leading a team of 17 mechanics and welders maintaining heavy mining equipment.

I am targeting office- or lab-based roles in process/metallurgical engineering, process simulation, data analytics, and engineering design within the metallurgical, mining and mineral-processing industries, including nickel, copper and gold. Based in East Java, Indonesia.
